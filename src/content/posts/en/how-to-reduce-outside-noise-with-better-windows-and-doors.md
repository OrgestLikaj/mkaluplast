Traffic, neighbours and city life can make it hard to relax at home. The good news: upgrading your windows and doors is one of the most effective ways to reduce noise indoors, especially if your current frames are old, poorly sealed or single-glazed.

Noise enters a building through gaps, weak glazing and low-quality sealing. Modern PVC and aluminium windows with proper gaskets, multi-point locking and quality glazing block sound far more effectively. In many cases, the biggest improvement comes from fixing air leaks and choosing the right glass configuration, not just from replacing the frame.

For the best results, combine a high-quality profile with professional installation and careful sealing around the window or door perimeter. If you live near a main road or in a busy area, ask about glazing options built for acoustic comfort.
