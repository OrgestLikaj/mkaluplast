Choosing between PVC windows and aluminium windows often comes down to what matters most: thermal efficiency, design, durability and budget. Both materials can deliver excellent results when paired with the right profiles and glazing, but they perform differently depending on the application and the type of building.

## PVC windows

PVC windows are known for strong thermal insulation, helping reduce heat loss in winter and keeping rooms cooler in summer. This supports lower energy use and a more stable indoor temperature. They are also a great option for homeowners who want a cost-effective upgrade without compromising on comfort.

## Aluminium windows

Aluminium windows, especially thermal-break aluminium systems, are ideal when you want slim frames, modern aesthetics and high structural strength. They are perfect for larger openings and contemporary architecture.

## Which one should you choose?

To get the best outcome, focus on the complete system: profile quality, sealing, installation, and double or triple glazing. If your priority is maximum warmth and energy efficiency, PVC is often the first choice. If you want a premium modern look with excellent performance, thermal aluminium is a top contender.
