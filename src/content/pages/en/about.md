MK AluPlast is a company with long-standing experience in the production and installation of aluminium systems, PVC products, security doors and other solutions for residential and commercial spaces.

Founded in 1999, the company began in aluminium processing and the production of security doors. From the very beginning we have been driven by a strong commitment to quality, safety and professional customer service — and by building long-term relationships with our clients.

Over the years we have continuously expanded our range. Alongside aluminium systems, we now produce and install PVC windows and doors with high thermal and acoustic insulation, as well as metal railings, shower cabins and customised solutions for private clients, businesses and construction projects.

Every product is crafted with care, precision and quality materials to ensure durability, aesthetics and long-term safety.
